# Code of Conduct

This project is run by amateur radio operators, for amateur radio operators.
The standard here is the one that already applies on the air: be courteous, be
patient with people who know less than you do, and assume the other operator is
acting in good faith until they prove otherwise.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion on the technical matter at hand. Disagreement about a design
  or a decode result is normal and welcome; disparaging the person is not.
- Help newcomers. Someone asking why their Scottie 1 decode slants is doing
  exactly what this project is for.
- Respect that contributors work on this in their own time, across time zones
  and languages, and that English is not everyone's first language.
- Give credit. If a fix came from someone's bug report or from another
  project's code, say so.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity,
  including their nationality, callsign, licence class, or lack of one.
- Publishing anyone's private information — including a home address derived
  from a callsign lookup — without their explicit consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or repeatedly ignoring a maintainer's
  request to drop a subject.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and any
other space where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Raise a concern with the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ssamjung2) — use a private channel if the matter
involves a person rather than a patch. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept confidential. A vulnerability in the code goes to
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that breach this document,
and may block accounts that repeat the behaviour after being asked to stop.
There is no committee here and no appeals process; this is a small project, and
the maintainer's judgement is what it has.

If this project grows enough to need a formal policy, the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) is the usual
choice and will replace this document.
